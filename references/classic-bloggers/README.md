## Applied synthesis

- Nearby subject-first personal portraits with readable face, skin and outfit. Background depth comes from layered surfaces, plants, furnishings or urban detail around the person, rather than shrinking the subject into a wide travel shot. Explicit user framing still wins.
- An intentional outfit/background color relationship and tactile surfaces. Choose a few believable details, not every decorative motif at once. Keep the user's supplied location or location reference when present.
- Five broad pose families per five-shot batch, while retaining the 24 flexible cues and recent-history rotation. Flow chooses exact limbs, support, camera placement and venue. A user-specified pose remains binding.
- Fine skin detail only at the scale visible in an ordinary portrait; localized highlights, soft expression creases and natural tonal variation. Keep complexion coherent across the face, neck and limbs. No generic porcelain skin, wax, uniform pore overlays, invented freckles or exaggerated imperfections. These are rendering directions, not claims that every source photo is unretouched.
- Preserve the user's exact figure and measurements. A thick build requires actual substantial volume, not a thin torso with just a larger bust. Outfit references supply design, not the model's body or projected garment width. Adapt sizing, drape and folds to the resolved figure; non-stretch material and loose clothing remain plausible.
- No creator name, face, body measurements or copied composition is inserted into the generation prompts. The reference list supplies general visual qualities only.
